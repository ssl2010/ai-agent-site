PRIVATE PERSONAL AUTOMATION

Sun's AI Agent

A private, owner-operated assistant that helps authorized users organize email, calendar, documents, travel, and expense workflows.

What it does

  • Creates concise email briefings and time-sensitive alerts.
  • Helps manage calendar events and travel itineraries.
  • Organizes authorized documents and expense receipts.
  • Performs mailbox actions only when enabled by the user.

Google data access

Features are enabled only after the user grants the relevant Google OAuth permissions. The app requests only the permissions needed for enabled features. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the user's Google Account.
